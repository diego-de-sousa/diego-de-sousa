# Diego de Sousa

**Full-stack developer & product designer** based in Galicia, Spain.

I build fast, accessible websites and SaaS products with a strong focus on design quality, performance, usability, and long-term maintainability.

My work sits at the intersection of **design and code**: I care about how digital products look, how they feel, how they perform, and how they are built.

## What I do

- High-performance websites and product sites
- SaaS products, dashboards, and internal tools
- Accessible, responsive user interfaces
- Frontend architecture with modern web technologies
- Design-to-code implementation with attention to detail
- UX, performance, and maintainability improvements

## Tech I work with

**Frontend:** Astro, Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS  
**Backend & services:** Supabase, Clerk, Stripe  
**Tooling:** Git, Vercel, Netlify, Figma, Obsidian

## Selected work

- [**unslump!**](https://github.com/diego-de-sousa/unslump) — a science-backed bilingual PWA for 25-minute movement breaks for desk workers.
- [**diegodesousa.com**](https://github.com/diego-de-sousa/diegodesousa) — my multilingual portfolio, built with Astro, Tailwind CSS, MDX, and a strong focus on accessibility and performance.
- **Floralia** — a gardening and home-growing app for organizing plants, tasks, and growing spaces.
- **Grandes Memorias** — lasting memorial pages connected through QR codes.

More public projects are being prepared.
